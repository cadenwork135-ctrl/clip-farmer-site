Clip Farmer - Privacy Policy Last updated: November 10, 2026 Clip Farmer is a private tool used only by the owner of the GTAVI Moments accounts to upload videos to the owner's own TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook accounts. What data it uses - The sign-in tokens for the owner's own accounts, provided by each platform when the owner logs in. These are stored only on the owner's personal computer and are used only to upload videos. - Basic profile information of the owner's own TikTok account (such as the account name), only to confirm the right account is connected. What it does NOT do - It does not collect, store or share data about any other users or viewers. - It does not sell or share any data with third parties. - It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising tracking. YouTube API Services Clip Farmer uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel (GTAVI Moments). By using Clip Farmer, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy From YouTube, Clip Farmer only uses the upload permission. It does not read, store or share any YouTube viewer data, comments, analytics or other users' information. The YouTube sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer. Deleting data and revoking access The owner can disconnect Clip Farmer at any time in the settings of each platform (for TikTok: Settings and privacy > Security and permissions > Apps and services). For YouTube/Google, access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions The owner can also delete the stored tokens from the computer. Clip Farmer keeps no copy of any data on any server, so once the tokens are deleted or access is revoked, no data remains.
